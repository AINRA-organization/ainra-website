AINRA World · the test network

    The test network, as its record states it

    Every part comes from the record. The root, its directory, the registrars and every passport are the ones the test network published. Each globe of dots is a registrar's signed status list, one dot per bit, 4,096 each, counted from the top. A passport's dot is full while it is in service and hollow once its registrar has switched it off; the small grey dots are bits not in use.

    The ring around each globe is that registrar's log, its entries in order. Each passport is a moon at its own entry; a hollow moon is switched off. All entries are hashed into one signed head, and a passport's proof is a few hashes that stand for the rest of the log.

    You are the verifier. This tab holds the directory, checked against the root's two keys. Each blue thread is a real check of one passport, run here by the verifier the site ships. It contacts nobody while it checks.

    The timeline replays the record in its own order, with the dates the record carries. Until 23 November 2026 the test network kept a fixed test date, so its early entries share two days in April.

    Drawn by us: where things sit and how they travel (the registrars along the orbit, the entries around each globe), the light, and the turning. It holds still when you choose something.
